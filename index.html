FAQ

Common questions about our technology

INITION has developed a patented controlled structure electrode production technology for lithium-ion batteries. This process fundamentally changes how electrodes are manufactured, eliminating the need for toxic solvents like NMP while achieving superior cell performance in terms of energy density and fast-charging capabilities.

The cost savings come from multiple factors: eliminating expensive toxic solvents and their recovery systems, dramatically reducing factory space requirements by a factor of 10, simplifying the production process, and lowering energy consumption. These combined efficiencies result in over 46% reduction in cell production costs for high performance markets.

Being chemistry agnostic means our electrode production technology works with any lithium-ion battery chemistry — whether it's NMC, LFP, NCA, solid-state, or emerging chemistries. Manufacturers aren't locked into a specific material system and can choose the optimal chemistry for their application.

Traditional electrode manufacturing relies on NMP (N-Methyl-2-pyrrolidone), a toxic and expensive solvent that requires costly recovery systems. INITION's patented dry-process technology produces electrodes without any solvents, and importantly, without relying on PFAS as alternatives.

Our technology opens battery production to a much broader range of industries. From emerging battery manufacturers to established players in automotive, energy storage, consumer electronics, and aerospace. The reduced factory footprint and elimination of toxic materials make it feasible for organisations that previously couldn't justify the costs.

Yes, INITION's controlled structure electrode production technology is fully patented. Our approach has been developed and validated to deliver the performance improvements we describe — including the 46% cost reduction, 20%+ energy density gains, and complete elimination of toxic solvents.
